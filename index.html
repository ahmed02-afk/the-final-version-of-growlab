01 · The method

Find. Connect. Grow. In that order.

We call it people as a service. People are busy every day but not moving: they have skills and ideas, but no clear plan and nobody they trust with the next step. We find what is really blocking a founder or a business, then bring in the right people to unblock it.

  1. Find
  2. Connect
  3. Grow

01 · Find

Find the real problem

The “sales problem” is often a message problem. We look under the surface first, and write it down in a Growth Brief.

Rule at this step

  • 1 · Diagnose first. Nobody sells a solution before we know the real problem.

02 · Connect

Connect the right people

Prepared introductions, agreed by both sides, with your first one by day 15 and a follow-up within 48 hours.

Rules at this step

  • 3 · Consent on both sides. Every introduction is prepared and agreed by both people. Profiles stay anonymous until then.
  • 4 · Follow up within 48 hours. After every introduction, we stay in the thread.
  • 5 · Vetted people only. Everyone is screened on their portfolio and in an interview.

03 · Grow

Grow step by step

Short sprints with a demo each time and a roadmap at day 30. You see what moved, and we adjust together.

Rules at this step

  • 2 · Roadmap or refund. No roadmap and at least one relevant introduction by the end of a Sprint? The fee comes back.
  • 6 · Clear prices. What’s on the price list is what you pay.

Six rules, every client, every time. Each one sits at the step where it applies. Our targets on every client: first shortlist about 10 days after the diagnosis, first introduction by day 15 of a Sprint.

04 · Questions

What leaders ask us before they start.

Growth still runs through me. How do you help me step back without losing control?

We map who owns what today, clarify roles, then bring in the hires or fractional people who can take over. The aim is a team that grows beyond you, with decisions that no longer wait on you.

How do you approach our first senior hires?

The role is defined from the diagnosis first, then we shortlist vetted people. You only meet candidates who fit, and both sides agree before any introduction.

Our people are capable, but teams don’t work well together. Where do you start?

With a diagnosis of where team, market and service lifecycle are out of line. Then we work alongside your leaders with targeted development sessions, 1:1 or in groups, and follow up on what changes.

Do you work with the leadership team, with managers, or both?

Both. 1:1 sessions for leaders, group sessions for teams, and follow-up in between so the work carries into daily practice.

Can you support a whole cohort rather than a single venture?

Yes. We support founders and develop their teams across a cohort, from the first diagnosis to introductions to the right experts and follow-up.

What do the founders in our program actually receive?

A clear diagnosis of what is holding each venture back, a roadmap, and introductions to vetted experts, talent or partners, with follow-up after each one.

Our IT organization is strong technically, but stretched by transformation. How do you help?

It starts with an IT Team Growth Check: where roles, ownership and collaboration are breaking under the change. Then we act on it through hiring, the right consultants, and development sessions for your leads.

How do you support teams through change, including bringing in AI?

We work alongside you through the transformation: clear roles as structures shift, support for the people delivering it, and, where AI really helps, the right experts to put it in place.

How is this different from a consulting firm?

We diagnose before we recommend anything. Then we stay and do the work with you: the right people, the right consultants, and follow-up until it moves. Results can also be priced as a success fee, agreed in writing first.

What commitment does it take from our leadership?

A 60 to 90 minute diagnostic session, a written Growth Brief, and short check-ins. We do the searching and the screening.

What guarantee do you offer?

If a Sprint ends without a roadmap and at least one relevant introduction, the fee comes back.

How do you vet the people you introduce?

Every person is vetted before we propose them, both sides agree before an introduction, and we stay in the conversation to follow up.

When is GrowLab not the right fit?

When there is no intent to act on what the diagnosis finds. We work best with leaders who move within 48 hours.
